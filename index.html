Triage Tiers & SLAs

Incidents are categorized into Priority (P) tiers based on their calculated score. Our recommended response SLAs are:

Score >= 80

P2 - Urgent
Score 60 - 79

P3 - Scheduled
Score 35 - 59
Moderate risk. SLA: 24 hours. Phishing attempt or port scan.
P4 - Routine
Score < 35
Low risk. SLA: 7 days. Informational advisories, low criticality.
